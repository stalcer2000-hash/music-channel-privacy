Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

What the app accesses. music-channel-stats requests read-only access to YouTube channel data and YouTube Analytics reports of the Google account that the owner signs in with. It reads aggregate statistics of the owner's own channel and videos.

How the data is used. The data is used only to build statistics reports for the owner. It is not sold, shared, published, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage. Reports are stored in a private repository and on the owner's computer. Access tokens are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never published.

Other users. The app has a single user, its owner. It does not collect data about other people.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which all stored tokens stop working.

Use of Google data. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. Questions can be raised via the issue tracker of this repository.
